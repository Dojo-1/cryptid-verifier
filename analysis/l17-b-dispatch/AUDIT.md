# Scope and audit record

The mathematical advance is a guarded dispatcher for the actual next bits
at a B exit. It closes the saved 108-step example and retains a precisely
specified A exit. There are eleven B rows and sixteen compositions with
C2/C5 entries. None asserts blank reachability or universal return.

The formal advance is narrower: six contextual finite paths have exact
`ReachesIn` declarations in `L17l.lean`, including their defined-transition
counts. Their outside tapes are arbitrary. `VALIDATION.md` records the
actual build and trust results. Read intervals come from the saved literal
traces and the finite symbolic paths; the Lean endpoints do not contain
a separate read-set predicate. The all-parameter dispatch table still
needs formal composition of these kernels and the earlier sweep lemmas.
A seventh formal declaration proves P_m00=0(10)^m0 for every m.

Evidence is bounded. Both literal implementations receive exactly the same
924 bit tapes, clocks and head states. They agree at every endpoint and on
911 full traces. The remaining 13 requests exceed the comparator's fixed
1000-step trace limit; their full endpoints and read extrema still agree.
Ninety-six cases put random context on both sides of a finite kernel.
Thirty-three translated inputs agree in three compiler modes, and 24
planted faults are detected. These paths share authorship and are not an
external reproduction. A passing mutation campaign measures those planted
faults, not all possible errors.

Discovery probes preceded `protocol.json`; the seed and caps apply to the
confirmatory C++ campaign. The initial all-trace transport request was
rejected; its conservative transition upper bound remains in the budget.
The finite-prefix test was corrected to include the exterior zero after
a final written one. The first Lean attempt hit the recursion-depth limit
on the 95-step path, which was raised locally before rechecking. These
failures are recorded, not counted as successful first attempts.

representation, implementation, test, metric and report, with explicit
