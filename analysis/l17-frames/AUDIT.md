ancestry ranks and a precise obstruction to affine scalar ranking. The literal

waggling or elongated display simplifies the machine. No claim of a global
stack bound, universal collapse to S or nonhalting survives this contribution.

## Strongest result and obstruction

The internal graph is a disjoint union of finite paths. A complete configuration
inside that interface has at most one internal ancestor at each step, and a
push entry is a root. This can reject a proposed reachable boundary whose unique
ancestry does not end at a compatible push entry. It does not handle outer S
steps or traversal through unsupported J/G states. The forward rank explicitly
allows an unresolved exit, so it cannot be used as a universal collapse proof.

The obstruction to replacing this hierarchy by an affine scalar is exact.
Allow one affine scalar per mode, bounded below. Its leading-N coefficients
must vanish. D2,D1,D12,G1 then imply incompatible inequalities; multipliers
2,3,3 yield11alpha_D<0 with alpha_D>0. The result does not exclude nonlinear,
lexicographic or history-sensitive candidates.
